## ** App Navigation Overview**

The multi-page Streamlit application is organized into the following core sections:
* **Welcome Hub (`app.py`):** The primary landing page providing system introductions, project branding, and direct navigation buttons across all application modules.
* **Professional Bio (`1_Bio.py`):** Showcases professional background, technical toolkits, visualization philosophy, coursework highlights, and interactive modal previewers for academic research posters and peer-reviewed publications.
* **EDA Gallery (`2_Charts_Gallery.py`):** Features comprehensive exploratory data analyses covering all **four research questions** across multiple distinct chart types, complete with plain-language reading guides and data-backed observations.
* **Interactive Dashboard (`3_Dashboard.py`):** Powered by reactive sidebar filters and linked visual cards allowing users to examine patient survival, geographic distribution, and environmental hazard co-occurrences.
* **Future Work & Clinical Ethics (`4_Future_Work.py`):** Outlines next steps and highlights important ethical considerations for utilizing visualizing human data.

## ** Research Questions Framework**

This application is structured around four primary research questions:
* **Demographic Makeup & High-Risk Subgroup Analysis:** Investigating global patient distributions across WHO regions, country diagnosis rates, age distributions, and demographic risk factors.
* **Environmental & Exposure Impacts:** Examining ambient air pollution, secondhand smoke, radon, and asbestos exposures, including co-occurrence patterns across cohorts.
* **Genetic Factors & Oncogenic Mutations:** Analyzing the prevalence of primary driver mutations (`EGFR`, `KRAS`, `ALK`, `TP53`, etc.) across histological subtypes (`NSCLC` vs. `SCLC`).
* **Population Disproportionality & Survivability:** Evaluating health disparities, age-gender intersections, and stage-dependent survival outcomes.

## **  Dataset Information**

* **Dataset Name:** Kaggle Global Lung Cancer Dataset (`lung_cancer_dataset.csv`)
* **Source Link:** [Kaggle Datasets](https://www.kaggle.com/)
* **Record Count:** 2,000 global patient records (satisfying the $>200$ rows project requirement) across 41 clinical and environmental attributes.
* **Data Preprocessing & Imputation:** Inspected for missing values and structural consistency using Pandas, implementing categorical age-bracket binning and binary indicator matrices for multi-hazard environmental exposure analyses.
* **Ethics Note:** *This dataset represents clinical oncology records compiled for research benchmarking. Because regional and demographic distributions reflect specific sampling structures, patterns shown in this application are descriptive only and should not be used for individual clinical diagnosis or broad epidemiological generalizations.*
